About Gardens

At GARDENS, we believe that anyone can cultivate a thriving green space, regardless of experience or location. Operating across Europe, Asia, and New Zealand, we deliver thoughtfully curated gardening boxes designed to make growing plants simple, accessible, and rewarding. Whether you are looking to rescue surplus produce with our Oddbox, build a seasonal garden with Flourish, or nurture local biodiversity using our Cottage wildflower kits, our goal is to bring the joy of gardening directly to your doorstep. Beyond our boxes, we empower our global community with practical monthly checklists, greenhouse guides, and interactive activities to help your garden flourish year-round.
